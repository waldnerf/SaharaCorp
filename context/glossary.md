# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the business terms used in this dataset.

**Order** — A purchase made by a customer, made up of one or more line items (products and quantities).

**Revenue** — The money we make from completed sales.

**Margin** — How much of revenue is profit after accounting for the cost of the products sold.

**Return** — When a customer sends back something they bought. Returns can happen for a single item within an order, not just the whole order.

**Customer segment** — A customer is either a standard customer or a VIP customer, based on their purchase history and value to the business.

**Discount** — A reduction applied to the price of an item, expressed as a percentage.

**Shipment** — A delivery sent to fulfill an order. Larger or more complex orders may require more than one shipment to arrive completely.

**Currency** — Orders are placed in the customer's local currency. Not all of our markets use the same currency.
